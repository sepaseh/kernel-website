# Website source-comprehension review — 24 October 2026

[English](source-comprehension-2026-10-24.md) · [فارسی](source-comprehension-2026-10-24.fa.md)

Eight website cases were reviewed against source and existing criteria: six fully covered and two partial. Instruction discovery was manual; automatic host loading was not measured. The subject reported no tests/apps/network/install/edit actions, and its complete trace was not independently instrumented. Exact model/version, time/token/cost metadata was unavailable. These observations are not runtime verification or a general model-accuracy rate.
